10. ANIMATION ARCHITECTURE & REBUILD BOTTLENECK AUDIT
================================================================================
- CRITICAL RENDERING HOTSPOTS (PERFORMANCE BOTTLENECK #1):
  1. RoseFiveLoader, RoseThreeLoader, RoseTwoLoader:
     - Implementation: Custom Ticker created via createTicker() calling setState(() {}) inside the callback.
     - Frame Rate: Ticks every frame (60 FPS or 120 FPS on High Refresh Rate displays).
     - Impact: Forces a full StatefulWidget rebuild of the entire parent dialog/loader subtree 60 to 120 times per second!
     - Solution: Remove setState(() {}) and replace with AnimatedBuilder, ListenableBuilder, or custom repaint listener on the CustomPainter.
  2. CropRecommendationLoader:
     - Implementation: Timer.periodic firing every 130ms calling setState(() {}).
     - Impact: Rebuilds full loader tree ~8 times per second during Gemini query execution.
  3. CultivationPlanningLoaderDialog:
     - Implementation: Timer.periodic firing every 1400ms updating stage indices with setState(() {}).

12. GPU BLUR & GLASSMORPHISM IMPACT MATRIX
================================================================================
- Element Inspected: GlassBlur (lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart) & GlassCard (lib/features/soil_analysis/widgets/glass_card.dart).
- GPU Pipeline Behavior:
  - BackdropFilter with ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: 14, sigmaY: 14) requires the Flutter engine to save a copy of the background scene buffer to an offscreen render target, apply a 2D Gaussian blur shader, and composite it back onto the framebuffer.
- RepaintBoundary Audit:
  - Most GlassCard instances in lists (e.g., MandiScreen, SoilAnalysisScreen, RiskAnalysisScreen) lack RepaintBoundary wrappers.
  - Result: Every scroll gesture or animation tick forces the GPU to re-render offscreen blur passes for dozens of cards simultaneously, leading to severe frame dropping (jank) on mid-range and budget mobile devices.
